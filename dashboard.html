High-Frequency Viva Voce Questions & Model Answers

Q1. Why is UDP latency lower under network congestion?

TCP enforces strict in-order delivery. When packets drop, TCP halts delivery and enters Retransmission Timeouts (Head-of-Line blocking). UDP drops packets permanently and passes surviving packets immediately without delay.

Q2. Why does throughput increase with larger packet sizes?

Due to header-to-payload amortization. An IPv4/TCP header is 40 bytes. In a 64-byte packet, the header consumes 62.5% of transmission bandwidth. In a 1460-byte MSS segment, the header is only 2.7%, yielding >97% useful data throughput.

Q3. What is the difference between Flow Control and Congestion Control?

Flow control protects the receiver from buffer overflow (advertised via rwnd). Congestion control protects the intermediate network routers from queue collapse (managed via cwnd using Slow Start and AIMD).

Q4. Why use a Paired t-test instead of Independent t-test?

Our experiment is a matched-pairs design. Trial TR_001 evaluates both TCP and UDP under identical background network conditions and packet sizes. A paired t-test tests within-pair differences, eliminating extraneous noise.
